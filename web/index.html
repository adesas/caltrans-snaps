SR-70 & SR-99 traffic camera recordings — Oroville, Yuba City, Marysville, Chico

This site keeps a rolling 3-day (72-hour) archive of Caltrans live traffic cameras along the Highway 70 corridor and Highway 99 through the southern Sacramento Valley — footage that is normally only viewable live and then lost. Pick a camera, choose any day and hour of the past three days, watch the recording, and export clips (e.g. to check traffic, weather, fog, fires, or an incident after the fact).

Cameras covered

  • SR-70 (Hwy 70): Feather River Blvd & Saddleback Dr (Marysville), Ramirez Rd (Yuba City), Cox Ln (Oroville)
  • SR-99 (Hwy 99): Skyway & Southgate Ln (Chico), Neal Rd (Durham), Hwy 162 / Oroville, Riego Rd (Nicolaus/Sutter)

Counties: Butte · Yuba · Sutter · Sacramento. Don't see your area? Request a county. Footage courtesy of Caltrans CCTV; this archive is independent and not affiliated with Caltrans.

Recordings are stored locally and served as HLS. Not affiliated with Caltrans.
