Traffic camera recordings — SR-70 · SR-99 · I-5 · I-80 · US-50 · SR-51 (Oroville to Folsom)

This site keeps a rolling 30-day archive of Caltrans live traffic cameras along the Highway 70 corridor and Highway 99 through the southern Sacramento Valley — footage that is normally only viewable live and then lost. Pick a camera, choose any day and hour of the past 30 days, watch the recording, and export clips (e.g. to check traffic, weather, fog, fires, or an incident after the fact — including collisions reported days or weeks later).

Cameras covered

  • SR-70 (Hwy 70): Ramirez Rd (Yuba City), Cox Ln (Oroville); Feather River Blvd & Saddleback Dr (Marysville) — currently offline
  • SR-99 (Hwy 99): Hwy 32 & Skyway & Southgate Ln (Chico), Neal Rd (Durham), Hwy 162 / Oroville, Riego Rd (Nicolaus/Sutter); south metro: Galt, Elk Grove (Dillard/Calvine/Florin Rd), Sacramento (MLK Jr Blvd, Fruitridge, I-5 junction)
  • I-5: Twin Cities & Cosumnes (Elk Grove), Florin, 43rd Ave, Richards, Airport–P St area, I-5↔I-80 and I-5↔Hwy-50 junctions (Sacramento)
  • I-80: Causley Bridge / Yolo Bypass side (West Capitol, Enterprise*, Reed), West Sac–Marysville corridor: Bryte Bend, Truxel, Northgate, Norwood, Watt, Greenback/Antelope *(some feeds pending Caltrans recovery)
  • US-50: Rancho Cordova → Folsom Point of Concern intersection sites — Manlove, Mayhew, Bradshaw, Routier, Mather Field, White Rock, Zinfandel, Sunrise, Hazel (one camera per site) + the Folsom end: Folsom Blvd, Prairie City Rd, E Bidwell St
  • SR-51: Watt Ave, Arden Way, El Camino Ave, Exposition Blvd, Hwy-160 ramp junction, T/N/E St ramp cluster (Sacramento)

Frequently asked

  • How far back can I watch? Every camera is recorded around the clock; any hour of the past 30 days is playable and exportable.
  • Can I download a clip? Yes — pick Clip-from-playhead or Whole-hour in Export; you get an MPEG‑TS file that plays in VLC (remux to MP4 with ffmpeg -i x.ts -c copy out.mp4).
  • Is there a camera per site? Each camera has its own page under /cams/, or use the 🗺 map to click-select a location.
